About Ratatouille Fox

A home-cooking website for people who love colourful vegetables, easy dinners and long evenings around the table.

Our story

Why a fox?

Foxes are clever, resourceful and good at making a feast out of whatever they find. That’s exactly how we like to cook: open the fridge, see what’s there and turn it into something delicious.

Ratatouille Fox began as a family notebook of dinners that worked on busy weeknights. Ratatouille was the first recipe in it, and it’s still the one we make most. Over time the notebook grew into this website.

Tested at home

Every recipe is cooked in an ordinary home kitchen with standard equipment before it is published.

Vegetables first

We build meals around seasonal produce, with plenty of vegetarian options and clear labels.

Clear instructions

We write methods that are easy to follow, with timings, temperatures and helpful tips.

How we work

From market to table

We shop at markets and ordinary supermarkets alike, so ingredients are easy to find. Recipes are written, cooked, adjusted and cooked again until they work reliably.

We follow recognised food safety guidance for temperatures and storage. Read more in our Editorial Policy.
