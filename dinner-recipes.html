Dinner recipes for every evening

Five dependable dinners we cook again and again, from a rustic one-pot ratatouille to lemony roasted fish. Each one is written for home kitchens and everyday ingredients.

Vegetarian · One pot

Rustic stovetop ratatouille

Serves 4 · 50 minutes

The quicker, chunkier cousin of the layered bake. Cooking each vegetable briefly before combining them keeps their shapes and flavours distinct.

Ingredients

  • 1 aubergine and 2 courgettes, cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 2 peppers, chopped
  • 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, thyme and basil

Method

  1. Fry the aubergine in half the oil until golden; set aside. Repeat with the courgettes.
  2. Soften the onion and peppers, add the garlic, tomatoes and thyme and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Return the vegetables, cover and cook gently for 20 minutes. Finish with basil and season to taste.
Vegetarian · 25 minutes

Green summer pasta with herbs

Serves 4 · 25 minutes

Bright, fresh and on the table in the time it takes to boil the pasta. Swap in whatever green vegetables you have.

Ingredients

  • 400 g short pasta
  • 2 courgettes, grated
  • 150 g peas
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • Grated hard cheese, olive oil and a handful of herbs

Method

  1. Cook the pasta, adding the peas for the final 2 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the courgette in olive oil with a pinch of salt for 4 minutes.
  3. Toss everything with lemon, cheese, herbs and a splash of pasta water until glossy.
Vegetarian · Make ahead

Roasted tomato & pepper soup

Serves 4 · 50 minutes

Roasting the vegetables first gives this soup a deep, sweet flavour. Serve it with toasted bread or a grilled cheese sandwich.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 2 red peppers, chopped
  • 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves
  • 700 ml vegetable stock
  • Olive oil, smoked paprika and basil

Method

  1. Roast the tomatoes, peppers, onion and garlic with oil and paprika at 200°C for 35 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a pan with the stock and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth, season and serve with basil.
Pescatarian · 35 minutes

Lemon & herb baked fish

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

A light, elegant dinner that cooks in a single tray. Choose any firm white fish fillet and the freshest herbs you can find.

Ingredients

  • 4 white fish fillets
  • 500 g small potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • Cherry tomatoes and green beans
  • Olive oil, parsley and dill

Method

  1. Toss the potatoes with oil and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes and beans, lay the fish on top with lemon and herbs.
  3. Roast for 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily and is cooked through.
Family favourite · One pan

Chicken & vegetable skillet

Serves 4 · 45 minutes

A cosy, saucy dinner made in one pan. Thigh fillets stay juicy, and the vegetables soak up all the savoury juices.

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 onion and 2 peppers, sliced
  • 200 g mushrooms
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • Garlic, paprika, thyme and olive oil

Method

  1. Season and brown the chicken in a large pan; set aside.
  2. Cook the onion, peppers and mushrooms until soft, then add garlic, paprika and thyme.
  3. Add the tomatoes, return the chicken and simmer for 20 minutes until cooked through (74°C / 165°F inside).

Food safety

Cook poultry until it reaches 74°C (165°F) in the thickest part, and fish until it flakes easily and is opaque.

Leftovers

Cool leftovers quickly, refrigerate within two hours and eat within three to four days.

Allergens

Check labels for allergens such as gluten, dairy, nuts and fish, and adapt recipes to suit your needs.
